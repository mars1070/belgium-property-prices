Belgium property prices: Statbel medians by commune, 2010-2026

One tidy CSV with the median sale price of houses and apartments for Belgium, its three regions, its provinces and every commune, by year since 2010 and for the first half of 2026. Source data: Statbel, republished under CC BY 4.0.

Columns

nis_code, level, region, name_fr, name_nl, name_en, property_type, year, period, median_price_eur, sales_count, q1_price_eur, q3_price_eur, source, license

  • level: national, region, province or commune.
  • property_type: HOUSE, HOUSE_CLOSED, HOUSE_DETACHED, APARTMENT.
  • period: Y for a full year, otherwise a half-year.
  • Communes carry their French, Dutch and English names, keyed by NIS code.
